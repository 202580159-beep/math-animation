A planet orbiting around the sine using sine and cosine.

Controls:
SPACE - Pause or Resume
UP / DOWN - Change Speed
LEFT / RIGHT - Change Orbit Radius

Mathimatics
x = centerX + orbitRadius * cos(angle)
y = centerY + orbitRadius * sin(angle)
